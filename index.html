Junnan Huang

Hello! I am an MPhil student in Microelectronics at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), advised by Prof. Xinyu Chen. My research focuses on computer architecture and efficient inference for large language models. Before joining HKUST(GZ), I earned my B.Eng. in Electronic Science and Technology at Tianjin University.

I'm currently exploring computer architecture, AI chips, and systems for efficient large language model (LLM) inference in the cloud and on edge devices.
